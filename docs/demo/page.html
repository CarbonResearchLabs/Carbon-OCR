Clean Water for a Growing Valley

A year of new pipes, cleaner rivers and lower bills

A record year for repairs

This year the district replaced more old pipe than in any year since it was founded. Crews worked through the spring floods and the dry summer to keep water flowing to every home on the east side of the river.

Leaks fell by a third across the network. The money saved on lost water went back into repairs, and the district kept its rates flat for the second year in a row.

What we finished

  • Twelve miles of new main under Mill Road and Cedar Avenue.
  • A second pump at the north reservoir.
  • Smart meters for four thousand homes.
  • A new lab for testing river water every week.

Water quality

Every sample taken this year met the state standard. The new lab now tests the river each week instead of each month, so problems are found days sooner.1
